ABOUT READY AT HOME

Sensible preparedness for ordinary households.

Ready at Home translates trusted official preparedness advice into simple household decisions. It is designed for families, couples, people living alone, renters, homeowners, pet owners and households with additional needs.

The guides focus on actions that are practical, affordable and easy to maintain. Strong marketing may get your attention; the advice inside is deliberately calm.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you start.

Which edition should I choose?

Choose the edition for the country where your household is based. The UK and US versions use different official sources, terminology, measurements, hazards and worksheet formats.

What does each purchase include?

Each regional edition is sold as one complete bundle containing two separate PDFs: the main guide and its printable household-planning workbook.

Can I buy only the guide?

Not at launch. The workbook is what turns the advice into a usable household plan, so the guide and workbook are supplied together.

Is this a survival manual?

No. It is a practical planning system for short household disruptions and emergencies, not a tactical, wilderness or weapons-based manual.

Do I need to buy everything at once?

No. The system begins with what you already own and shows how to fill the most important gaps gradually.

Is this professional emergency advice?

No. Always follow emergency services, local authorities, healthcare professionals and current official alerts. The guides help you prepare; they do not replace professional advice.
