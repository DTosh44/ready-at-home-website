QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

About the US Edition.

Is the US Edition available now?

Yes. The complete US guide and printable US Letter workbook are available together as an instant digital download.

What will the purchase include?

One bundle containing two separate digital PDFs: the full US guide and a printable workbook formatted for US Letter paper.

Why is it separate from the UK Edition?

US households use different agencies, emergency information, terminology, measurements, regional-hazard advice and paper sizes. Those differences deserve more than a spelling change.

Will it work for renters and apartments?

Yes. The guide is being designed for apartments, rented homes, homeowners and households with limited storage.

Will I need to buy everything at once?

No. The system starts with what you already own and prioritizes the gaps that matter most.

Is this professional emergency advice?

No. Always follow emergency services, local authorities, health professionals and current official alerts. The guide helps you prepare; it does not replace professional advice.
